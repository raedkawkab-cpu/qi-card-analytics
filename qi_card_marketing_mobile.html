🏅 Named Tier — Qi Platinum
Automatic enrollment for all Champions. Visible badge in the Qi Card app. Two sub-tiers: Platinum (all 271K Champions) and Platinum Elite (top 1,000 by spend, each over 10M IQD lifetime). Different badge colors. Different benefit levels. The tier itself is the marketing — people spend to maintain it.
🛡️ 15-Day Inactivity Alert — CRM Rule
No campaign needed — just a system rule. Any Platinum member without a transaction in 15 days triggers an automated personalized message: "We haven't seen you use your Qi Card lately — here's an exclusive offer for this week." In Q4→Q1 data, 21,252 Champions degraded to At Risk. This rule intercepts them before the 30-day mark.
🌍 International Priority — FX Margin Driver
Champions spend 626B IQD in USD-billed transactions. FX margin at 2% = ~12.5B IQD/yr estimated across all segments, with Champions driving the majority. Market: "Preferred FX rate for Platinum members." Even a 0.1% improvement in FX capture on their USD volume is hundreds of millions of IQD in margin. This is simultaneously a retention tool and a direct margin driver.
🎁 Milestone Cashback — Surprise Mechanics
After every 100th transaction: 5,000 IQD surprise cashback. Not announced in advance — surprise increases emotional impact vs. expected reward. Data: Champions avg 57 transactions. Monthly average is ~8 transactions. At current pace, top Champions would hit milestones every 12 days. Cost: fully predictable from frequency data.
🤝 Platinum Referral Program
Champion earns 3,000 IQD for every friend they refer who activates and completes 3 transactions within 30 days. Qualifier: referral must be net new to Qi Card. Champions have proven spending behavior — their networks are the highest-quality acquisition channel. Data from merchant intel shows Champions concentrated at SHEIN, PUBGMOBILE, ALIBABA — communities where peers influence peers.
💳 Card Upgrade — Rafidain → Corporate
134,266 Champions still on Rafidain Classic (avg 2.85M IQD spend). MC Corporate Champions avg 7.2M IQD — 2.5× higher. The card is not causing the higher spend — both reflect the same client profile — but upgrading provides: higher credit limit, higher interchange rate for Qi Card, better FX terms, and a status signal. Offer: "Upgrade to Platinum Corporate Card — no annual fee for first year."

Monthly CRM email showing each Loyal client their progress: transactions this month, trajectory to Platinum, and one offer for the next month. Simple, data-driven, personalized. Sends automatically from CRM. No manual effort at scale. Keeps Qi Card top-of-wallet for their next transaction decision.

4. Needs Attention — Salary-Cycle Timing
206,288 clients · 37.0B IQD · 81.4% in 2–5 txn bucket · send everything on the 25th–28th of the month
P1 — Timing is the Strategy
⏰ Salary-Day SMS Campaign
Every outbound message to Needs Attention goes out between the 25th and 28th of each month. That is when salary arrives in Iraq. The message: "Your Qi Card is ready for this month. Exclusive offer this week: [merchant offer]." This is not just about the offer — it is about being present at the moment the client has money and is thinking about spending. Generic timing (mid-month, random) misses the window entirely.
💰 Monthly Spend Summary — 1st of Month
On the 1st of each month: "Last month you spent [X IQD] with Qi Card and saved [Y IQD] on [merchant]." Spending summaries serve two functions: they remind clients the card is working for them (reducing churn), and they create an aspiration — if they spent more, they would save more. Data already available from transaction history; automation is the only requirement.
🛍️ E-Commerce Offer Match
50.2% of Needs Attention volume is e-commerce. Their top channels overlap with SHEIN, DIGITAL ZONE, TIKTOK. Send a merchant-specific offer at salary timing: "5% back at SHEIN this week — expires Sunday." Category match dramatically outperforms generic cashback for this group because it aligns with existing behavior rather than asking for new behavior.
🔔 60-Day Auto-Escalation Rule
Internal CRM rule (no client-facing action): any Needs Attention client who reaches 60 days without a transaction is automatically moved to the At Risk campaign track. Real-time monitoring. At current At Risk growth rate (+17,942 new clients in April alone), this rule prevents the compounding problem at source. The rule costs nothing to implement and saves the campaign cost of recovering fallen At Risk clients later.
💬 SMS — 25th–28th only 📧 Monthly summary — 1st 📱 App notification
📈
Revenue margin impact: Retaining 45–55% from degrading to At Risk = 92,800–113,500 clients protected. At 179K IQD avg spend and 1.5% interchange = 2.5B–3.0B IQD in annual interchange preserved. More importantly, each Needs Attention client who doesn't become At Risk saves Qi Card the cost of a recovery campaign (800–2,500 IQD/client) plus the value lost during inactivity.

6. New Customers — 7-Day Onboarding Window
53,431 clients · 283K IQD avg spend · 78.3% e-commerce · 86.9% made 1 transaction — the window closes in 7 days
P2 — Automation Required
📅 Day 1 — Welcome + What Qi Card Can Do
Immediate after first transaction: personalized welcome message. "Your Qi Card is now active. Here's what you unlocked:" + top 3 merchant categories matching their first transaction type. Not generic benefits — personalized to what they actually did. 78.3% of New Customers transact e-commerce first: the message should reference e-commerce merchants.
📅 Day 3 — First Merchant Offer
"Exclusive Qi Card offer at [merchant in their first category]: 3% back on your next purchase, expires in 4 days." Match to their first transaction merchant category. If they used SHEIN: send a SHEIN offer. If they used PUBGMOBILE: send a gaming offer. Category match converts at 3× the rate of generic offers because it confirms the card works where they already spend.
📅 Day 7 — Second Transaction Incentive
"Make your second Qi Card purchase this week — get 300 IQD back automatically." Simple. Specific. 7-day window. 86.9% of New Customers made only 1 transaction. The second transaction is the only conversion goal. Everything before day 7 is building up to this ask. After the second transaction, they automatically move to Regular Customers and enter the next campaign track.
⚠️ Fix the Acquisition Collapse — Separate Investigation
New client acquisition collapsed 92% from October (64,543 clients) to April (4,922 clients). This is not a marketing problem — it is a pipeline problem. The October spike must be diagnosed: Was there a product launch? A bank partnership? A government program? A competitor failure? Replicating October's acquisition performance is worth more to Qi Card than any campaign in this document. This requires a separate investigation with the operations and partnerships teams.
💬 SMS Day 1, 3, 7 (automated) 📱 In-app notification
📈
Revenue margin impact: 60% second-transaction conversion = 32,059 clients. At 283K IQD per additional transaction and 1.5% interchange = 136M IQD from the second transaction alone. More importantly: each client who completes the second transaction has a 3× higher 90-day retention rate than single-transaction clients. The onboarding sequence is the single highest-ROI marketing investment in the Activation tier.

🟢 6+ Transactions (12,494) — Personal + High Budget
