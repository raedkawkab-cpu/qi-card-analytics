🎯 Five Priority Actions

👑 Named VIP Program — Qi Platinum
Every Champion enrolled automatically. Named tier, visible in app. Personal relationship manager for top 1,000 (>10M IQD spend). Credit limit increase without request. First access to new Qi Card products. Activate using Export 14 account list.
🛡️ 15-Day Inactivity Alert — P0 Rule
Any Champion without a transaction in 15 days → immediate CRM alert to account manager. Data shows 21,252 Champions degraded to At Risk in one quarter. This single rule prevents the most expensive churn in the portfolio. Zero budget required — CRM system configuration only.
💳 Product Upgrade — Rafidain → Corporate
134,266 Champions are on Rafidain Classic (avg 2.85M IQD each). MC Corporate Champions avg 7.2M IQD — 2.5× higher. Targeted card upgrade campaign. Even 10% conversion = 13,400 clients with potentially doubled spend.
🌍 International Premium Benefits
43.1% of Champion volume is e-commerce. Top merchants include Alibaba, SHEIN, TRIP.COM, Air Arabia. Preferential FX rate, travel insurance, airport lounge access. Makes Qi Card the default card for international spend — the highest-value transaction channel.
🤝 Ambassador Referral Program
Champion earns 2,000 IQD for each referred friend who activates and transacts 3+ times. Champions trust Qi Card enough to spend 57 transactions on average. Their networks are the lowest-cost acquisition channel available.
🎁 Milestone Surprise Rewards
Auto-trigger: 100th transaction → 5,000 IQD cashback surprise. 6-month anniversary → personalized message + benefit. 1-year → exclusive gift or offer. Creates emotional loyalty beyond transactional mechanics. Zero incremental cost at scale.
